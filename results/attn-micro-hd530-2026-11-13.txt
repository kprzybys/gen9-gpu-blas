## Reading (docs/attention-gpu.md, sections 4 and 7)
- exp: 13.3 Gop/s precise, 44.0 Gop/s native_exp (= exp with -cl-fast-relaxed-math). Softmax of Qwen2.5-1.5B at
  d = 3584 (6.6e8 exp) costs 50 ms precise / 15 ms native, i.e. 3-5 % / 1 % of the predicted 1.05-1.58 s:
  not a bottleneck; use native_exp (or native_exp2 with log2(e) folded into the scale).
- SLM: 177 GB/s for consecutive float4 and for broadcast (all lanes one address), about 92 % of 64 B/clock per
  subslice x 3 subslices at 1.0 GHz (192 GB/s). Stride 2 float4: 96 GB/s (half of each 64 B access wasted).
  At 300 GFLOPS (150 G multiply-adds/s) SLM supplies about 1.2 B per multiply-add, so a 2-byte K/V element read from
  SLM must serve at least 2 multiply-adds from registers; broadcast reads cost the same as consecutive ones.
