Conclusions:
- GEMM at M = 1–16: ViennaCL 30–135 ms (1.1–6 GB/s of weights) -- its GEMM computes full-height tiles (like our old
  path with packing), so the time hardly depends on M; libgen9blas 1.0–11 ms (13–22 GB/s): 2.9–14.6× faster.
- GEMV (M = 1, the counterpart of skinny GEMM in ViennaCL): tie at 4096² and 4096×11008 (1.0×); libgen9blas slower at
  11008×4096 (0.8×) and 2048² (0.7×; 1.09 vs 0.78 ms) -- to be checked (part of it is the call from Python, ~0.03–0.1 ms).
- Instead of M separate GEMVs: libgen9blas M = 16 (FP32 4096²: 4.06 ms) costs 1.3× of one ViennaCL GEMV (3.15 ms);
  16 GEMV calls in ViennaCL are ~50 ms, i.e. ~12× longer.
