Conclusions:
- Repeating the test: ViennaCL today gives vectors/GEMV +10–25% vs 2021, dGEMM NN/NT ~2× (34 vs 17.8), TN/TT +15–25% --
  newer driver/IGC compiler and a different platform; order of magnitude consistent, the method works on our hardware.
- GEMM: libgen9blas sGEMM 317–326 GFLOPS vs 22–60 (5–14×), dGEMM ~55 vs 17–34 (1.6–3.2×; vs the 2021 result: 3.1–3.7×);
  FP16 (absent in ViennaCL) 584–618 GFLOPS. libgen9blas is even across all transpositions, ViennaCL loses 1.5–2.7× on TN/TT.
- Vectors and GEMV (memory-bound): tie (0.95–1.08×) except for two weaknesses to fix: dCOPY 17.4 vs
  22.9 GB/s (0.76×) and dGEMV-T (for us gemv N in FP64, rows) 21.6 vs 25.3 GB/s (0.85×); FP16 COPY/GEMV 14–15 GB/s.
